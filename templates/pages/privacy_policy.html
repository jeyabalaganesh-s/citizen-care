Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Citizen Care is a software development and testing project. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you visit our website and use our services.

1. Information We Collect

Citizen Care does not currently operate a public customer messaging or customer data collection service through WhatsApp.

Our WhatsApp Cloud API integration is used only for development, testing and personal use with our own registered phone number.

We do not intentionally collect or maintain customer personal information through this WhatsApp integration.

2. How We Use Information

Information processed through our development and testing activities is used only for:

  • Testing WhatsApp Cloud API connectivity.
  • Testing webhook functionality.
  • Testing message delivery and status notifications.
  • Maintaining and improving the application.
  • Ensuring the security and reliability of our services.

3. WhatsApp Communications

Citizen Care uses the WhatsApp Business Platform provided by Meta for personal development and testing purposes.

The integration is currently configured to use our own phone number. We do not use it to send marketing messages, promotional messages or unsolicited messages to customers.

We do not intentionally collect, store or maintain customer contact lists, customer profiles or customer conversations through this integration.

Meta may provide technical information such as phone numbers, message IDs, timestamps and message status notifications through its webhook system when applicable. Any information received during testing is used only for technical testing and is not intended to be used for customer profiling or marketing.

WhatsApp and Meta process information according to their own applicable terms and privacy policies.

4. AI-Assisted Services

Any AI-assisted functionality, if enabled, is intended for application development and testing. Information submitted to an AI service may be processed by that provider to generate responses.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. We do not share customer information for advertising or marketing purposes.

Technical information may be processed by service providers such as hosting providers and Meta when necessary to operate and test the application.

6. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect application data and technical information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

Testing information is retained only for as long as necessary for development, debugging and security purposes.

7. Data Deletion

If you believe that personal information related to you has been collected through our services, you may contact us to request access to or deletion of that information, subject to applicable legal requirements.

You can also visit our Data Deletion page .

8. Third-Party Services

Our application may use third-party services, including Meta WhatsApp Cloud API, hosting providers and AI service providers for development and testing.

These services operate under their own privacy policies and terms of service.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization where required by applicable law.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our application or data practices change. The latest version will always be published on this page.
