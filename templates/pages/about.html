About Citizen Care

Making Citizen Voices Heard

Citizen Care provides a simple and transparent platform for citizens to report concerns, submit complaints and track their progress.

Our Purpose

We aim to make complaint reporting accessible, organized and transparent through technology. Citizens can submit issues and receive updates without unnecessary complexity.

What We Do

Citizen Care connects citizens and complaint-handling teams through a centralized digital platform.

  • Complaint status tracking
  • WhatsApp communication support
  • Organized complaint management
